# Profile

I am a **Computer Engineering student at the École Nationale d’Ingénieurs de Sfax (ENIS)** with a strong interest in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, LLMs, AI Agents, Big Data, and Distributed Systems**.

My background combines a strong foundation in **mathematics, physics, algorithms, and software engineering** with hands-on experience building real-world projects. Through my academic work, internships, and personal projects, I have worked across the full development stack, from data collection and model development to backend services, APIs, distributed infrastructure, and user-facing applications.

I have completed **two internships in an ERP company**, where I gained professional experience working on real software projects. I worked with technologies such as **Python, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, Angular, Node.js, Express, databases, REST APIs, authentication, and Git**, while learning how to work with existing codebases, solve technical problems, develop new features, and collaborate within a development environment.

My main focus today is **AI Engineering**. I have developed projects involving **PyTorch, CNNs, Transformers, Computer Vision, OCR, Document AI, XGBoost, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, LLMs, and AI Agents**.

I have also explored **Big Data and distributed systems**, working with technologies such as **Hadoop, HDFS, MapReduce, Kafka, Docker, Redis, Cassandra, and RabbitMQ**.

Some of my projects include:

* **LLM From Scratch** — implementing the fundamental components of an LLM, including tokenization, embeddings, attention, Transformer blocks, and text generation.
* **WebSocket From Scratch** — implementing WebSocket communication over TCP to understand the protocol and networking layer.
* **AI Fitness Coach** — real-time pose estimation with MediaPipe and OpenCV, combined with geometric analysis, finite-state repetition detection, fuzzy logic, and voice feedback.
* **Intelligent Invoice Data Extraction** — combining Tesseract OCR and the Donut Vision Transformer to extract structured information from invoices.
* **Photovoltaic Project Management Platform** — a full-stack application integrating Angular, Node.js, MongoDB, FastAPI, and XGBoost for intelligent task-duration prediction.
* **Big Data Twitter Analysis System** — a distributed Hadoop pipeline using Selenium, HDFS, MapReduce, Flask, and Angular.
* **Agent Lab / Agent LangChain** — experimenting with LLM agents, tools, memory, RAG, LangGraph, multi-agent systems, and orchestration.
* **HPIS — Human Performance Intelligence System** — an AIoT architecture combining wearable data, Kafka, Cassandra, Redis, RabbitMQ, machine learning, and AI agents.

A key part of my engineering philosophy is:

> **I like building things from scratch.**

I don't want to only know how to use a technology. I want to understand **what happens underneath the abstraction**, implement its core concepts myself, understand its limitations, and then use that knowledge to build larger and more reliable systems.

My long-term goal is to become an **AI Engineer capable of designing complete intelligent systems**, combining **AI models, LLMs, agents, real-time data, distributed systems, software engineering, and IoT** into practical applications.
