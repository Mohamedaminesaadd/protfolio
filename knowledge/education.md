## Education

> I am currently pursuing an **Engineering Degree in Computer Engineering (Génie Informatique)** at the **École Nationale d’Ingénieurs de Sfax (ENIS)** in Tunisia.
>
> Before joining the engineering cycle, I completed a **Mathematics and Physics preparatory cycle**, which gave me a strong foundation in mathematics, physics, algorithms, and problem-solving.
>
> During my engineering studies, I progressively specialized in **Artificial Intelligence and software engineering**, with a particular interest in:
>
> * Machine Learning
> * Deep Learning
> * Computer Vision
> * Natural Language Processing
> * Large Language Models
> * AI Agents
> * Big Data
> * Distributed Systems
> * Backend Engineering
> * IoT and AIoT
>
> My education has been strongly project-oriented. I try to complement theoretical courses with practical implementations, which is why I have built projects involving **PyTorch, Transformers, LLMs, Computer Vision, Hadoop, Kafka, WebSockets, and AI agents**.
>
> One of the most important aspects of my education is that I don't want to learn technologies only at the API level. I like to go deeper and **build simplified versions from scratch** to understand the underlying concepts. For example, I have worked on an **LLM from scratch**, a **WebSocket implementation from scratch**, and progressively built my understanding of neural networks, Transformers, and agent architectures.
>
> This combination of a strong mathematical foundation, engineering education, and hands-on projects has shaped my approach to AI: **understand the fundamentals, implement them, and then use them to build complete systems.**
